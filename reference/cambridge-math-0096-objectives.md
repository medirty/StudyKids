# Cambridge Primary Mathematics 0096 — learning objectives (paraphrased)

Source: Cambridge Primary Mathematics Curriculum Framework 0096 (current edition, first teaching 2020),

Fetched 2026-11-19. Wording is paraphrased (not verbatim); every code is listed. If a summary is unclear, check the PDF.

TWM (all stages): TWM.01 Specialising, .02 Generalising, .03 Conjecturing, .04 Convincing, .05 Characterising, .06 Classifying, .07 Critiquing, .08 Improving

## Stage 1
- Nc: 1Nc.01 count 0–20 one-to-one; 1Nc.02 recognise familiar patterns to 10 without counting; 1Nc.03 estimate to 20, check; 1Nc.04 count on/back in 1s, 2s, 10s (0–20); 1Nc.05 even/odd as every other number; 1Nc.06 describe object sequences
- Ni: 1Ni.01 number names 0–20; 1Ni.02 addition as counting on/combining; 1Ni.03 subtraction as counting back, take away, difference; 1Ni.04 complements of 10; 1Ni.05 estimate, add, subtract within 20; 1Ni.06 doubles to double 10
- Nm: 1Nm.01 recognise local currency
- Np: 1Np.01 zero as none; 1Np.02 compose/decompose 10–20; 1Np.03 compare and order 0–20; 1Np.04 ordinals 1st–10th
- Nf: 1Nf.01 split into two equal/unequal parts; 1Nf.02 half = one of two equal parts; 1Nf.03 half as operator (whole answers); 1Nf.04 halves combine to wholes
- Gt: 1Gt.01 time-unit language; 1Gt.02 days of week, months; 1Gt.03 time to hour and half hour
- Gg: 1Gg.01 sort 2D shapes by sides/curvature; 1Gg.02 length vocabulary; 1Gg.03 sort 3D shapes by faces/edges; 1Gg.04 mass vocabulary; 1Gg.05 capacity vocabulary; 1Gg.06 2D vs 3D; 1Gg.07 shapes identical when rotated; 1Gg.08 select measuring instruments
- Gp: 1Gp.01 position and direction language
- Ss: 1Ss.01 answer non-statistical questions with categorical data; 1Ss.02 record/represent categorical data (objects, lists, tables, Venn/Carroll, block graphs, pictograms); 1Ss.03 describe data more/less/most/least

## Stage 2
- Nc: 2Nc.01 count 0–100; 2Nc.02 unfamiliar patterns to 10 without counting; 2Nc.03 estimate to 100; 2Nc.04 count in 1s, 2s, 5s, 10s (0–100); 2Nc.05 even/odd characteristics; 2Nc.06 recognise, describe, extend sequences
- Ni: 2Ni.01 number names 0–100; 2Ni.02 link addition and subtraction; 2Ni.03 complements of 20 and multiples of 10; 2Ni.04 add/subtract 2-digit without regrouping; 2Ni.05 multiplication as repeated addition and arrays; 2Ni.06 division as sharing and grouping; 2Ni.07 1, 2, 5, 10 times tables
- Nm: 2Nm.01 value and notation of local currency; 2Nm.02 compare coin/note combinations
- Np: 2Np.01 digit value by position, zero placeholder; 2Np.02 compose/decompose/regroup 2-digit; 2Np.03 compare and order 2-digit; 2Np.04 ordinal numbers; 2Np.05 round to nearest 10
- Nf: 2Nf.01 split into four equal/unequal parts; 2Nf.02 quarter = one of four equal parts; 2Nf.03 half and quarter as division; 2Nf.04 half, quarter, three-quarters as operators; 2Nf.05 relative size/equivalence of 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, 1; 2Nf.06 combine wholes, halves, quarters
- Gt: 2Gt.01 order and compare time units; 2Gt.02 read/record time to 5 minutes (digital, analogue); 2Gt.03 interpret calendars
- Gg: 2Gg.01 2D shapes: regular polygons, sides, vertices; 2Gg.02 circle centre and boundary; 2Gg.03 length as fixed distance, estimate, measure; 2Gg.04 draw and measure lines; 2Gg.05 3D shapes: faces, edges, vertices; 2Gg.06 mass as quantity of matter; 2Gg.07 capacity as maximum contents; 2Gg.08 2D/3D shapes in objects; 2Gg.09 horizontal/vertical lines of symmetry; 2Gg.10 shape repetitions in a full turn; 2Gg.11 angle as turn (whole/half/quarter, clockwise/anticlockwise); 2Gg.12 measuring scale as continuous number line
- Gp: 2Gp.01 position and direction to describe movement; 2Gp.02 sketch reflection in vertical mirror line
- Ss: 2Ss.01 investigations with categorical data; 2Ss.02 record/represent categorical data, justify; 2Ss.03 describe data, similarity and variation
- Sp: 2Sp.01 language of regular and random patterns; 2Sp.02 chance experiments with two outcomes

## Stage 3
- Nc: 3Nc.01 estimate up to 1000; 3Nc.02 count in steps of 1-digit, 10s, 100s (0–1000); 3Nc.03 even/odd to sort numbers; 3Nc.04 object as unknown in +/−; 3Nc.05 linear sequences, term-to-term rule; 3Nc.06 spatial patterns from constant +/−
- Ni: 3Ni.01 number names 0–1000; 3Ni.02 commutative and associative addition; 3Ni.03 complements of 100, multiples of 10/100; 3Ni.04 add/subtract up to 3 digits with regrouping; 3Ni.05 multiplication–division relationship; 3Ni.06 commutative and distributive multiplication; 3Ni.07 1–6, 8, 9, 10 times tables; 3Ni.08 multiply up to 100 by 2–5; 3Ni.09 divide up to 100 by 2–5; 3Ni.10 multiples of 2, 5, 10 up to 1000
- Nm: 3Nm.01 decimal-point money notation; 3Nm.02 add/subtract to give change
- Np: 3Np.01 digit value up to 3 digits; 3Np.02 multiply whole numbers by 10; 3Np.03 compose/decompose/regroup 3-digit; 3Np.04 compare and order with =, >, <; 3Np.05 round to nearest 10 or 100
- Nf: 3Nf.01 fractions as equal parts of one whole; 3Nf.02 part–whole depends on relative size; 3Nf.03 fractions as equal parts of a set; 3Nf.04 fraction as division; 3Nf.05 fractions as operators (incl. third, tenth); 3Nf.06 equivalence (halves, quarters, fifths, tenths); 3Nf.07 add/subtract same-denominator fractions within one; 3Nf.08 compare and order unit and same-denominator fractions
- Gt: 3Gt.01 choose time units; 3Gt.02 read/record time (digital 12-hour, analogue); 3Gt.03 interpret timetables; 3Gt.04 time vs time interval
- Gg: 3Gg.01 2D shapes, regular vs irregular polygons; 3Gg.02 estimate/measure in cm, m, km; 3Gg.03 perimeter and area concepts; 3Gg.04 draw lines, rectangles, squares; perimeter and area on grids; 3Gg.05 3D shapes: identify, sort, name, sketch; 3Gg.06 mass in g and kg; 3Gg.07 capacity in ml and l; 3Gg.08 pictures/diagrams of 3D shapes; 3Gg.09 horizontal/vertical lines of symmetry; 3Gg.10 compare angles with a right angle; 3Gg.11 instruments for length, mass, capacity, temperature
- Gp: 3Gp.01 position, direction, movement with cardinal points; 3Gp.02 reflection in horizontal or vertical mirror lines
- Ss: 3Ss.01 investigations with categorical and discrete data; 3Ss.02 record/represent (Venn/Carroll, tally, frequency tables, pictograms, bar charts), justify; 3Ss.03 interpret similarity and variation
- Sp: 3Sp.01 chance language (will, will not, might); 3Sp.02 chance experiments and results

## Stage 4
- Nc: 4Nc.01 count in steps of 1-digit, 10s, 100s, 1000s incl. negatives; 4Nc.02 generalisations adding/subtracting even and odd; 4Nc.03 objects/shapes/symbols as unknowns; 4Nc.04 linear and non-linear sequences; 4Nc.05 square-number patterns
- Ni: 4Ni.01 number names above 1000 and below 0; 4Ni.02 estimate, add, subtract up to 3 digits; 4Ni.03 associative multiplication; 4Ni.04 all times tables 1–10; 4Ni.05 multiply up to 1000 by 1-digit; 4Ni.06 divide up to 100 by 1-digit; 4Ni.07 multiples and factors; 4Ni.08 divisibility by 2, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100
- Np: 4Np.01 digit value by position; 4Np.02 multiply/divide by 10 and 100; 4Np.03 compose/decompose/regroup whole numbers; 4Np.04 compare and order positive and negative numbers; 4Np.05 round to nearest 10 … 100 000
- Nf: 4Nf.01 more parts = smaller parts; 4Nf.02 fraction as division (unit fractions, three-quarters); 4Nf.03 unit fractions as operators; 4Nf.04 equivalent proper fractions; 4Nf.05 add/subtract same-denominator fractions; 4Nf.06 percentage as parts per hundred; 4Nf.07 compare and order proper fractions
- Gt: 4Gt.01 convert between time units; 4Gt.02 read/record time 12- and 24-hour, analogue; 4Gt.03 interpret timetables; 4Gt.04 time intervals in days–years and in s/min/h not bridging 60
- Gg: 4Gg.01 shapes from combining shapes, tessellation; 4Gg.02 perimeter and area, compound shapes; 4Gg.03 rectangles/squares on grids, derive formulae; 4Gg.04 area of irregular shapes on grids; 4Gg.05 2D faces of 3D shapes; 4Gg.06 match nets to 3D shapes; 4Gg.07 horizontal, vertical, diagonal lines of symmetry; 4Gg.08 estimate, compare, classify acute, right, obtuse angles; 4Gg.09 read scales using fractions
- Gp: 4Gp.01 position/movement with cardinal and ordinal (8) points; 4Gp.02 coordinates, first quadrant; 4Gp.03 reflect in horizontal/vertical mirror lines on grids
- Ss: 4Ss.01 plan and conduct investigations; 4Ss.02 record/represent incl. dot plots, justify; 4Ss.03 interpret within/between data sets, variation
- Sp: 4Sp.01 chance language (maybe, likely, certain, impossible); 4Sp.02 chance experiments small and large trials

## Stage 5
- Nc: 5Nc.01 count in constant steps incl. negatives; 5Nc.02 symbols for two unknowns; 5Nc.03 find any term in a linear sequence; 5Nc.04 square and triangular number patterns
- Ni: 5Ni.01 estimate, add, subtract integers incl. negatives; 5Ni.02 choose laws of arithmetic to simplify; 5Ni.03 order of four operations; 5Ni.04 multiply up to 1000 by 1- or 2-digit; 5Ni.05 divide up to 1000 by 1-digit; 5Ni.06 prime vs composite; 5Ni.07 divisibility by 4 and 8; 5Ni.08 square numbers 1–100
- Np: 5Np.01 tenths and hundredths; 5Np.02 multiply/divide whole numbers by 10, 100, 1000; 5Np.03 multiply/divide decimals by 10, 100; 5Np.04 compose/decompose/regroup decimals; 5Np.05 round 1-decimal-place to whole
- Nf: 5Nf.01 fraction as division (unit, ¾, tenths, hundredths); 5Nf.02 proper fractions as operators; 5Nf.03 improper fractions ↔ mixed numbers; 5Nf.04 equivalence fractions / 1dp decimals / percentages; 5Nf.05 add/subtract fractions same or multiple denominators; 5Nf.06 multiply/divide unit fractions by whole numbers; 5Nf.07 percentages of shapes; 5Nf.08 compare/order decimals, fractions, percentages; 5Nf.09 add/subtract decimals same places; 5Nf.10 multiply 1dp numbers by 1-digit; 5Nf.11 proportion vs ratio
- Gt: 5Gt.01 intervals under one second; 5Gt.02 time zones; 5Gt.03 intervals bridging 60; 5Gt.04 intervals as decimals or mixed units
- Gg: 5Gg.01 isosceles, equilateral, scalene triangles; 5Gg.02 perimeter and area; same perimeter different areas; 5Gg.03 compound shapes from rectangles and squares; 5Gg.04 3D shapes in different orientations; 5Gg.05 nets of a cube; 5Gg.06 reflective symmetry to complete patterns; 5Gg.07 acute, right, obtuse, reflex; 5Gg.08 angles on a straight line = 180°
- Gp: 5Gp.01 relative position of coordinates; 5Gp.02 plot points to form shapes, first quadrant; 5Gp.03 translate shapes on grids; 5Gp.04 reflect in horizontal/vertical mirror lines
- Ss: 5Ss.01 investigations (categorical, discrete, continuous); 5Ss.02 represent incl. waffle diagrams, frequency diagrams, line graphs, dot plots; 5Ss.03 mode and median; 5Ss.04 patterns and sources of variation
- Sp: 5Sp.01 likelihood and risk; 5Sp.02 equally likely vs more/less likely; 5Sp.03 chance experiments and simulations

## Stage 6
- Nc: 6Nc.01 count in steps incl. fractions/decimals, negatives; 6Nc.02 letters for varying quantities; 6Nc.03 position-to-term rule; 6Nc.04 square numbers to generate terms
- Ni: 6Ni.01 estimate, add, subtract integers; 6Ni.02 laws of arithmetic and order of operations; 6Ni.03 brackets alter order; 6Ni.04 multiply up to 10 000 by 1- or 2-digit; 6Ni.05 divide up to 1000 by 1- or 2-digit; 6Ni.06 common multiples and factors; 6Ni.07 divisibility by 3, 6, 9; 6Ni.08 cube numbers 1–125
- Np: 6Np.01 digit value to thousandths; 6Np.02 multiply/divide whole numbers and decimals by 10, 100, 1000; 6Np.03 compose/decompose/regroup decimals to thousandths; 6Np.04 round 2dp to nearest tenth or whole
- Nf: 6Nf.01 fraction as division (proper, improper); 6Nf.02 proper and improper fractions as operators; 6Nf.03 simplest form; 6Nf.04 equivalence fractions/decimals/percentages; 6Nf.05 add/subtract fractions different denominators; 6Nf.06 multiply/divide proper fractions by whole numbers; 6Nf.07 percentages of shapes and wholes (1%, multiples of 5%); 6Nf.08 compare/order decimals, fractions, percentages; 6Nf.09 add/subtract decimals same or different places; 6Nf.10 multiply decimals by 1- and 2-digit; 6Nf.11 divide decimals by whole numbers; 6Nf.12 direct proportion; 6Nf.13 equivalent ratios
- Gt: 6Gt.01 convert decimal ↔ mixed-unit time intervals
- Gg: 6Gg.01 classify and sketch quadrilaterals; 6Gg.02 parts of a circle; 6Gg.03 area of right-angled triangles; 6Gg.04 compound 3D shapes; 6Gg.05 capacity vs volume; 6Gg.06 nets for cubes, cuboids, prisms, pyramids; 6Gg.07 area vs surface area; 6Gg.08 rotational symmetry order ≤ 4; 6Gg.09 classify, estimate, measure, draw angles; 6Gg.10 angles in a triangle = 180°; 6Gg.11 construct circles of given radius/diameter
- Gp: 6Gp.01 coordinates (integers, fractions, decimals) in four quadrants; 6Gp.02 shapes on coordinates, all quadrants; 6Gp.03 translate on coordinate grids; 6Gp.04 reflect in vertical, horizontal, diagonal lines; 6Gp.05 rotate 90° about a vertex
- Ss: 6Ss.01 plan, conduct, predict for investigations; 6Ss.02 represent incl. pie charts, scatter graphs, dot plots; 6Ss.03 mode (incl. bimodal), median, mean, range; 6Ss.04 patterns, variation, check predictions
- Sp: 6Sp.01 probability and proportion language; 6Sp.02 mutually exclusive events; 6Sp.03 some probabilities only by large trials; 6Sp.04 chance experiments and simulations, predict and analyse frequencies
